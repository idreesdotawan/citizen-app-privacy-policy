Privacy Policy

BürgerApp Deutschland for Android · Effective 30 September 2026

In short: the app only asks Android for Internet access. It has no ads and no account. Anything you enter to book an appointment is used to handle that booking and nothing else.

Who we are

BürgerApp Deutschland (“the app”) is provided by I.S Tech Company, Global, like Germany , pakistan, india , USA and Uk . Now special for Germany (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of personal data processed through the app.

Information we handle

WhatWhy
Booking details you type in, such as name, contact details, chosen office, service, date and time To create and manage your appointment
Content loaded from our server: offices, services, news, public holidays, emergency numbers To show you up-to-date information
Technical data in server logs, such as IP address, device type and request time To deliver content, keep the service secure and fix errors
Settings and saved items stored on your phone To remember your choices. This data stays on your device.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect advertising identifiers.

App permissions

  • Internet: required to load information and send your booking to our server.

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

How we use it

  • To run the app and its appointment booking feature.
  • To keep the service secure and find technical problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Under the GDPR our legal bases are performance of a request you make (Art. 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interest in a secure and working app (Art. 6(1)(f)), and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Sharing and hosting

The app communicates with our backend server, which is run by a hosting provider that processes data on our behalf. We share personal data with others only when the law requires it or when you have asked us to, for example when a booking must be passed to the office you chose.

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics tools.

Storage and retention

Booking data is kept only as long as needed to handle the appointment and any legal retention period. Server logs are deleted after a short period. Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you,
  • correct or delete it,
  • restrict or object to its processing,
  • give you a copy in a portable format,
  • withdraw consent you have given, with effect for the future.

You may also complain to your data protection supervisory authority. In Germany, find yours via the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. We will announce significant changes in the app.
